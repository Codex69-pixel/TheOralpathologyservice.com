Oral & Maxillofacial
Pathology Services

The Oral Pathology Service provides specialised diagnostic interpretation of oral and maxillofacial specimens — supporting clinicians across Kenya and East Africa with timely, accurate, and accessible pathology services.

Our Mission

To strengthen oral and maxillofacial diagnostic capacity across Kenya and underserved regions by reducing diagnostic delays, supporting effective clinical decisions, and improving patient care outcomes through accessible, specialist pathology. Accurate diagnosis enables better clinical outcomes — for every patient, in every setting.

FC Path (SA) Oral Pathology
Our Lead Specialist

Dr. Muthoni Ndonga

BDS (UoN) · MChD (UWC) · FC Path (SA) Oral Pathology

Dr. Muthoni Ndonga is a Dental Surgeon and Specialist Oral and Maxillofacial Pathologist based in Nairobi, Kenya, with more than 10 years of experience in oral healthcare and advanced diagnostic pathology. She completed specialist training in Oral Pathology at Kenyatta National Hospital and the University of Nairobi Dental Hospital, followed by specialist licensure from the Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council (KMPDC). Through The Oral Pathology Service, she focuses on improving access to specialist oral histopathology and cytopathology services across Kenya and the broader East African region.

Dr. Ndonga holds a Master of Dental Surgery in Oral Pathology from the University of the Western Cape, Cape Town, and a Fellowship in Oral Pathology from the College of Pathologists of South Africa. She currently serves as a Consultant Oral Pathologist and Lecturer at the University of Nairobi, training the next generation of dental specialists.
