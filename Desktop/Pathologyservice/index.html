Our Mission

Our mission is to strengthen oral and maxillofacial diagnostic capacity across Kenya and underserved regions by reducing diagnostic delays, supporting effective clinical decisions, and improving patient care.

What We Do

Specialist Oral Pathology Services

We provide specialist diagnostic pathology services for diseases of the oral cavity, jaws, salivary glands, and associated maxillofacial structures.

Histopathological Evaluation

Microscopic evaluation of oral and maxillofacial lesions, including inflammatory, infectious, odontogenic, benign, potentially malignant, and malignant conditions.

Routine & Complex Reporting

Clear, structured, and detailed diagnostic reports for standard cases, complex differentials, and referral consultations — written to support direct clinical action.

Advanced Testing & Diagnostic Services

Collaboration with accredited laboratories (Dr Kalebi Labs) for immunohistochemistry, molecular testing, microbiology, and other E.g. Ki67, S100 e.t.c.

Multidisciplinary Approach

We encourage submission of clinical history, photographs, and imaging to support thorough clinicopathological correlation and the most accurate diagnosis possible.

Second Opinions & Consultative Pathology

Independent expert review of challenging cases or prior diagnoses — providing specialist insight to support diagnostic clarity and guide patient management decisions.

Case Tracking & Clinical Enquiries

Track submitted cases, submit additional clinical information, and request report updates or expedited review through our secure online system.

How It Works

Our Simple 4-Step Process

From specimen submission to diagnostic report — a streamlined process designed for clinical efficiency.

1

Specimen Submission

Submit biopsy specimens with the completed request form and relevant clinical history. Download the PDF form or complete it online.

2

Pathologic Evaluation

Each specimen is meticulously examined by a board-certified oral pathologist with specialised expertise in oral and maxillofacial disease.

3

Diagnostic Report

Receive a concise, structured report detailing gross and microscopic findings, diagnosis, differential diagnoses, and clinical recommendations.

4

Clinical Support

Our team is available for direct clinician discussions, follow-up consultations, and additional guidance to support your patient management.

Meet Our Lead Pathologist

Dr. Muthoni Ndonga

BDS (UoN)  ·  MChD (UWC)  ·  FC Path (SA) Oral Pathology

Dr. Muthoni Ndonga is a Dental Surgeon and Specialist Oral and Maxillofacial Pathologist based in Nairobi, Kenya, with more than 10 years of experience in oral healthcare and advanced diagnostic pathology.

She completed her specialist recognition training in Oral Pathology at Kenyatta National Hospital and the University of Nairobi Dental Hospital. Following this, she received specialist and consultant licensure from the Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council (KMPDC).

Through her initiative, The Oral Pathology Service, she focuses on improving access to specialist oral histopathology and cytopathology services across Kenya and the broader East African region — providing clinicians with precise, timely, and clinically relevant diagnostic reports to support better patient outcomes.
