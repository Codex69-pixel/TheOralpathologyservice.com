How It Works

Our Simple 4-Step Process

From specimen submission to diagnostic report — a streamlined process designed for clinical efficiency.

1

Specimen Submission

Submit biopsy specimens with the completed request form and relevant clinical history. Download the PDF form or complete it online.

2

Pathologic Evaluation

Each specimen is meticulously examined by a board-certified oral pathologist with specialised expertise in oral and maxillofacial disease.

3

Diagnostic Report

Receive a concise, structured report detailing gross and microscopic findings, diagnosis, differential diagnoses, and clinical recommendations.

4

Clinical Support

Our team is available for direct clinician discussions, follow-up consultations, and additional guidance to support your patient management.

Our Lead Specialist

Dr. Muthoni Ndonga

BDS (UoN) · MChD (UWC) · FC Path (SA) Oral Pathology

Dr. Muthoni Ndonga is a Dental Surgeon and Specialist Oral and Maxillofacial Pathologist based in Nairobi, Kenya, with more than 10 years of experience in oral healthcare and advanced diagnostic pathology. She completed specialist training in Oral Pathology at Kenyatta National Hospital and the University of Nairobi Dental Hospital, followed by specialist licensure from the Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council (KMPDC).

Through The Oral Pathology Service, she focuses on improving access to specialist oral histopathology and cytopathology services across Kenya and the broader East African region.
